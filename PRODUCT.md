# Product

## Register

product

The authenticated workspace (`/app`) and the interactive demo (`/demo`) are product surfaces: design serves an analyst's task. The public website (`/`, `/platform`, `/methodology`, `/pricing`, …) is a brand surface and is designed in the brand register.

## Users

- **Primary: ESG and compliance consultants** serving small and mid-sized companies. One consultant handles several client companies, collects evidence from annual reports, sustainability reports and policies, and prepares gap reports. They work at a desk on a laptop or external monitor, often with the source PDF open beside them, under deadline pressure before a reporting date.
- **Secondary: investment and research analysts** comparing disclosures across companies and periods.
- **Secondary: reporting teams at mid-sized companies** preparing for requests from customers, banks and auditors.

The job: turn hundreds of pages of disclosures into a defensible view of what is supported, what is missing, what conflicts, and what to do next, with every conclusion traceable to a page.

## Product Purpose

Veridion turns company disclosures into traceable intelligence: every material finding is linked to its evidence, every gap has an explanation, and every recommendation has a clear rationale.

The core workflow: upload documents → assess a versioned requirement set → inspect evidence → compare peers → export a reviewable report. Success is a consultant who trusts a finding enough to put it in front of a client, because they clicked through to the exact passage and saw why it was reached — and who saves hours of manual cross-referencing doing it.

## Brand Personality

**Discreet, exacting, transparent.** A private research institution translated into software.

- Authoritative, never aggressive. Confident, never boastful.
- Analytical, never sterile. Minimal, never empty.
- Unusually transparent about how conclusions are reached and where the system is uncertain.

Emotional goal: the calm confidence of a senior analyst — "I can defend this."

## Anti-references

- Generic AI startup and SaaS templates: purple-to-blue gradients, glowing neural networks, robots, brains, sparkles, "Unlock the power of AI".
- Crypto dashboards and decorative KPI walls filled with meaningless numbers ("94% compliant", "10,000 sources analysed").
- Endless rounded cards, identical three-column feature grids, glassmorphism, floating glass cards, cursor-following glow, scroll hijacking, particles.
- Fake customer logos, testimonials, certifications or usage statistics. Fake terminal windows.
- Chatbot-first interfaces. The signature experience is the Evidence Trail, not a chat box.

## Design Principles

1. **Every conclusion is one click from its source.** If a finding, number or chart cannot be traced to a page and passage, it does not ship.
2. **Absence of evidence is not proof of non-compliance.** Language, status design and empty states must keep that distinction visible.
3. **Show uncertainty, don't hide it.** Confidence, weak evidence, model disagreement and comparability limits are first-class UI, not footnotes.
4. **Precision over decoration.** Hierarchy comes from type, spacing and tone. If an element exists only because it looks fashionable, remove it.
5. **Honest about maturity.** Never imply customers, certifications, offices or capabilities that do not exist; label sample data as fictional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA throughout: 4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text and UI boundaries, visible focus states, full keyboard operation of tables, panels and dialogs.
- Status is never communicated by colour alone: every status has a distinct glyph and a text label.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; animations never delay access to information.
- Semantic HTML, labelled controls and icons, readable document navigation, evidence panels that become drawers on small screens.
