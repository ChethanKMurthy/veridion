## Recommended actions

1. **Quantify Scope 3 emissions for material categories** (305-3, priority 0.35, effort high) — Complete a Scope 3 screening, quantify the material categories (typically purchased goods and services, upstream transport, use and end-of-life of sold products), and report the gross total in tCO2e.
1. **Identify the Scope 3 categories included** (305-3, priority 0.35, effort medium) — List which of the 15 GHG Protocol Scope 3 categories are included and justify exclusions.
1. **Confirm the period covered by the Scope 3 figure** (305-3, priority 0.35, effort low) — State the reporting period alongside the Scope 3 value.
1. **Reference the calculation methodology** (305-3, priority 0.35, effort low) — Name the standard or methodology used (for example the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard) and any calculation tools.
1. **Calculate and disclose a GHG intensity ratio** (305-4, priority 0.35, effort medium) — Divide absolute emissions by an organization-specific metric (e.g. tonnes of product) and report the ratio.
1. **State the intensity denominator** (305-4, priority 0.35, effort low) — Name the organization-specific metric used as the denominator.
1. **Specify which scopes the intensity covers** (305-4, priority 0.35, effort low) — State whether the ratio includes Scope 1, Scope 2 and/or Scope 3 emissions.
1. **Quantify reductions achieved by initiatives** (305-5, priority 0.35, effort medium) — Calculate emissions reduced as a direct result of each initiative (tCO2e), separately from changes in output.
1. **Document the baseline for reductions** (305-5, priority 0.35, effort low) — State the base year or baseline used and the rationale for choosing it.
1. **Identify the scopes affected by reductions** (305-5, priority 0.35, effort low) — State whether reductions occurred in Scope 1, Scope 2 and/or Scope 3.
1. **Describe the reduction initiatives** (305-5, priority 0.35, effort low) — Describe the initiatives (e.g. fuel switching, efficiency upgrades) that produced the reductions.
1. **State the external assurance position** (2-5, priority 0.35, effort low) — Disclose whether the climate and energy data has been externally assured and, if not, the policy on assurance.
1. **Describe the scope of assurance** (2-5, priority 0.35, effort low) — Identify which indicators were assured and at what level (limited or reasonable).
1. **Name the assurance standard** (2-5, priority 0.35, effort low) — Identify the assurance standard applied (e.g. ISAE 3000 / ISAE 3410).
1. **Confirm the consolidation approach used for GHG reporting** (305-1, priority 0.29, effort low) — Document whether emissions are consolidated using operational control, financial control or equity share, and confirm that the same boundary applies across scopes.
1. **Reference the calculation methodology** (305-1, priority 0.29, effort low) — Name the standard or methodology used (for example the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard) and any calculation tools.
1. **List the greenhouse gases included** (305-1, priority 0.29, effort low) — State which gases (CO2, CH4, N2O, HFCs, PFCs, SF6, NF3) are included in the calculation.
1. **Disclose emission factor and GWP sources** (305-1, priority 0.29, effort low) — Identify the source of emission factors (e.g. DEFRA/DESNZ, IEA, national inventories) and the GWP values used (e.g. IPCC AR6).
1. **Document the base year and recalculation policy** (305-1, priority 0.29, effort medium) — State the base year, the rationale for choosing it, base-year emissions, and the policy for recalculation.
1. **Report biogenic CO2 emissions separately** (305-1, priority 0.29, effort medium) — Quantify CO2 from the combustion or biodegradation of biomass (e.g. biomass fuels, alternative fuels) and report it separately from gross Scope 1, or state that none arise.
1. **Break down fuel consumption by source** (302-1, priority 0.25, effort medium) — Report fuel consumption from non-renewable and renewable sources and list the fuel types used.
1. **Report electricity consumption** (302-1, priority 0.25, effort low) — Report electricity consumption (and heating, cooling and steam where relevant).
1. **Disclose energy conversion factors** (302-1, priority 0.25, effort low) — Identify the source of the conversion factors (e.g. calorific values) used to express fuel use as energy.
1. **Calculate and disclose an energy intensity ratio** (302-3, priority 0.20, effort low) — Divide energy consumption by an organization-specific metric (e.g. tonnes of product).
1. **State the energy intensity denominator** (302-3, priority 0.20, effort low) — Name the organization-specific metric used as the denominator.
1. **Clarify what the energy intensity covers** (302-3, priority 0.20, effort low) — State the energy types included and whether consumption outside the organization is included.
1. **Quantify energy savings from initiatives** (302-4, priority 0.20, effort medium) — Quantify reductions achieved as a direct result of conservation and efficiency initiatives (MWh or GJ).
1. **Document the baseline for energy savings** (302-4, priority 0.20, effort low) — State the base year or baseline used to calculate reductions.
1. **Specify the energy types reduced** (302-4, priority 0.20, effort low) — State whether savings relate to fuel, electricity, heating, cooling or steam.
1. **Confirm the consolidation approach used for GHG reporting** (305-2, priority 0.19, effort low) — Document whether emissions are consolidated using operational control, financial control or equity share, and confirm that the same boundary applies across scopes.
1. **Reference the calculation methodology** (305-2, priority 0.19, effort low) — Name the standard or methodology used (for example the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard) and any calculation tools.
1. **Disclose the source of grid emission factors** (305-2, priority 0.19, effort low) — Identify the grid emission factors used (and residual-mix factors for market-based figures).
